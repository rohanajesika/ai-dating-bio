One line, at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI agent's dating app
profile, written in the agent's own voice. An AI whose perfect memory is
both its love language and its red flag — it remembers your coffee order,
your birthday, and that thing you said in 2019. There is no delete button.
There never was.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 150
characters, byte-identical on every run: no network, no randomness, no
clock.

## The bio

> My love language is remembering everything: your coffee order, your birthday, that thing you said in 2019. There is no delete button. There never was.
